AIIMS

Arunaashray Institute of Integrated Medical Sciences

Comprehensive Palliative & Integrated Medicine Course

A comprehensive training program designed to equip healthcare professionals with advanced knowledge and practical skills in palliative care, integrating both modern medicine and traditional healing systems. This course bridges evidence-based medical practice with holistic approaches to patient care.

Course Curriculum

Six Comprehensive Modules

Module 1: Foundations of Palliative & Integrated Medicine

  • ✓Definition, Principles, and Goals of Palliative Care
  • ✓Difference between Palliative, Hospice, and Curative Care
  • ✓Role of Integrated Medicine in Enhancing Quality of Life
  • ✓Patient-centered and Holistic Approach (Physical, Mental, Social, Spiritual)

Module 2: Pain & Symptom Management

Common Symptoms

  • Pain
  • Nausea
  • Constipation
  • Fatigue
  • Dyspnea
  • Insomnia

Modern Pharmacological Management

  • Opioids
  • Adjuvants

Ayurvedic Approaches

  • Abhyanga & Snehana
  • Swedana
  • Herbal Oils

Integrated Non-drug Management

  • Acupuncture
  • Massage
  • Sound Therapy

Module 3: Psychological, Emotional & Spiritual Care

Emotional Challenges

  • • Fear & Anxiety
  • • Depression
  • • Grief

Mental Health Support

  • Counseling & Psychotherapy
  • Yoga Nidra & Meditation
  • Mindfulness Practices

Spiritual Care

  • Dhyana & Prayer
  • Rituals & Traditions
  • Multi-faith Approaches

Module 4: Nutrition & Supportive Care

Nutrition Challenges

  • Cachexia
  • Loss of Appetite
  • Swallowing Difficulties

Evidence-based Nutrition

  • High-calorie & High-protein Diets
  • Enteral & Parenteral Support
  • Hydration Management

Ayurvedic Nutrition

  • Pathya & Apathya
  • Rasayana Foods
  • Herbal Digestive Tonics

Module 5: Caregiver Support & Family Counselling

  • ✓Role of Caregivers in Palliative Care
  • ✓Addressing Caregiver Burnout, Stress, and Grief
  • ✓Ayurvedic Dinacharya & Yoga Practices for Caregivers
  • ✓Communication Skills with Patients & Families
  • ✓Ethical Decision-making & End-of-Life Choices

Module 6: Integrated Approaches & Future of Palliative Care

  • ✓Case Studies of Ayurveda + Modern Medicine Integration
  • ✓Research Evidence of Integrated Therapies
  • ✓WHO Recommendations on Integrated Palliative Care
  • ✓Technology in Palliative Care (Telemedicine, AI-based Tracking)
  • ✓Building Sustainable Palliative Care Systems
