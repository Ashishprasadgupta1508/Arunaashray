About Arunaashray Health Care Foundation

Healthcare as a right, not a privilege

Our Story

Founded by Er. Aruna Shrivastava, Arunaashray Health Care Foundation was established with a singular vision: to transform healthcare accessibility in economically disadvantaged communities. What began as a compassionate initiative has grown into a comprehensive healthcare movement serving thousands across multiple states.

Our organization specializes in supporting individuals affected by cancer and age-related health challenges, providing not just medical care but also emotional support and holistic wellness to patients and their families. We believe healthcare is a fundamental human right, not a privilege reserved for the wealthy.

🎯

Our Mission

To create a sustainable and accessible healthcare framework that provides medical assistance, awareness programs, and emotional support for individuals and families affected by cancer and age-related health challenges.

👁️

Our Vision

To build a future where no individual is denied quality healthcare and where holistic well-being is recognized as a fundamental right, not a privilege.

❤️

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, and Excellence. We remain committed to transparency, accountability, and serving with dignity. Every decision we make is guided by the needs of those we serve.
