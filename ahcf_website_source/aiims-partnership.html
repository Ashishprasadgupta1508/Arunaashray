AIIMS Partnership

Bridging excellence in healthcare with accessibility

Our AIIMS Collaboration

Arunaashray Health Care Foundation has established a strategic partnership with AIIMS (All India Institute of Medical Sciences) to provide world-class medical expertise and treatment to underserved communities. This collaboration represents our commitment to bringing advanced medical care to individuals who otherwise would not have access to such specialized services.

Through this partnership, patients benefit from consultation, diagnosis, and treatment protocols developed by some of India's most renowned medical professionals. Our aim is to ensure that financial constraints do not become a barrier to quality healthcare.

Success Story: AIIMS Jodhpur Collaboration

Online Training on Home-Based Palliative Care

🏥 AIIMS Jodhpur × Arunaashray Health Care Foundation

Arunaashray Health Care Foundation (AHCF), in collaboration with AIIMS Jodhpur, successfully organized a 6-day online training program on Home-Based Palliative Care aimed at strengthening community-level palliative care delivery across India.

The program brought together healthcare professionals, nurses, and community health workers from different regions of the country, enabling them to enhance their clinical knowledge and compassionate care practices without geographical limitations.

📅 Program Duration

6 Days (2nd – 7th June 2025)

Time: 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM (Daily)

Mode: Online (Pan-India Accessibility)

🎯 Key Objectives

  • ✓ Build capacity in home-based palliative care
  • ✓ Improve pain & symptom management
  • ✓ Strengthen communication & ethics

📚 Training Content

  • ✓ Fundamentals of palliative care
  • ✓ Symptom management protocols
  • ✓ Case discussions & scenarios

🌍 Program Impact

The training program enhanced professional competence and confidence of participants, equipping them with practical tools to deliver quality, compassionate, and dignified care in home and community settings. By leveraging digital platforms, the initiative ensured inclusive access to expert knowledge, reinforcing AHCF's commitment to making quality healthcare education accessible to all.
