Our Featured Programs

AHCF runs two flagship programs that are transforming healthcare delivery in underserved communities. These initiatives showcase our commitment to both direct healthcare services and knowledge empowerment through innovative training approaches.

Active Programs

🚐

Mobile Health Camps

Bringing Healthcare to Remote Areas

Objective: Provide basic healthcare services to remote villages lacking medical infrastructure.

Activities:

  • Free medical checkups and consultations
  • Blood pressure and blood sugar screening
  • Diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses
  • Health education on hygiene and nutrition
  • Referral services for serious cases

Impact: Over 5,000 beneficiaries treated annually

Training Programs

Online Health Awareness and Skill Development

Objective: Provide accessible online health awareness and skill development courses to empower individuals with knowledge for better health decisions.

Activities:

  • Online health awareness courses on various health topics
  • Skill development workshops for community health workers
  • Live interactive sessions with healthcare experts
  • Self-paced learning modules for flexibility
  • Certification programs for participants

Impact: 2,000+ people trained in health awareness and skills development
