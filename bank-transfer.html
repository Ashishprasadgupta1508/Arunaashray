Direct bank transfer - Secure and reliable donation method

Bank transfer is one of the most secure and straightforward ways to support Arunaashray Health Care Foundation. Whether you're making a one-time donation or setting up a regular contribution, bank transfer provides a transparent and traceable method of supporting our healthcare initiatives.

How to Transfer

  1. Log in to your online banking account or mobile banking app
  2. Select "Fund Transfer" or "NEFT/RTGS Transfer" option
  3. Enter the account details provided above
  4. Specify the donation amount
  5. Add a reference note: "Donation to AHCF" (optional)
  6. Verify all details and confirm the transaction
  7. You will receive a confirmation receipt via email or SMS

Benefits of Bank Transfer

  • Secure: Your transaction is fully encrypted and protected
  • Traceable: Complete transparency with transaction records
  • Tax Benefit: Eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G
  • Receipt: Automatic receipt generation for tax purposes
  • Flexible: One-time or recurring donations supported
  • No Charges: Most banks offer free NEFT/RTGS transfers

Tax Benefits

Arunaashray Health Care Foundation is a registered NGO under Section 12A of the Income Tax Act. All donations made to AHCF are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G.
