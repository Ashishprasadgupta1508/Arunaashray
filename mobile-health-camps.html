🏥 Health Checkup Camp – Second Session

National Cancer Institute Hospital & AHCF Collaboration – Rewa District Hospital Initiative

Comprehensive Health Checkups & Follow-up Care

Following the successful completion of the earlier program, a second session of the Health Checkup Camp was organized at the same hospital, ensuring continued medical support and follow-up care for the community. The camp was conducted by Arunashray Health Care Foundation with the objective of providing comprehensive health checkups, early disease screening, and medical guidance, with a special focus on cancer-related evaluation and supportive care.

Medical Supervision

The camp was held under the supervision of Dr. Abhishek Shrivastava, who actively participated in patient assessment and guided individuals regarding further investigations, treatment options, and palliative support where required.

Services Provided

  • General health checkups
  • Patient registration and clinical evaluation
  • Screening for cancer-related symptoms
  • Medical consultation and counseling
  • Follow-up guidance for previously registered patients

Community Impact

The second session witnessed significant participation, reflecting increased awareness and trust among the local population. Many patients returned for follow-up, while new patients also benefited from early health evaluation and expert consultation.
