Our Services

Comprehensive healthcare, support, and healing for every stage of life

Arunaashray Health Care Foundation: A Beacon of Hope and Healing

At Arunaashray Health Care Foundation, we are committed to providing comprehensive healthcare support, awareness, and assistance with a special focus on cancer care, geriatric health, patient counseling, and deaddiction programs.

As a dedicated non-profit organization, we work tirelessly to ensure that quality healthcare is accessible to all—regardless of financial or social circumstances.

🧬 Cancer Care & Support

At Arunaashray, we provide holistic and compassionate cancer care, supporting patients and families at every stage of the journey.

  • Early detection & cancer screening programs
  • Emotional and psychological counseling
  • Financial assistance for underprivileged patients
  • Survivor care and palliative support

💻 E-Second Opinion by ACC (Arunaashray Cancer Care)

Our E-Second Opinion service enables patients to consult expert oncologists remotely, helping them make informed treatment decisions without travel barriers.

  • Remote consultation with cancer specialists
  • Access to expert medical guidance
  • Support regardless of location or income

👴 Geriatric Care

We address the complex healthcare needs of elderly individuals suffering from chronic illnesses and age-related conditions.

  • Regular health checkups and screenings
  • Assisted living and mobility support
  • Mental health counseling for depression & dementia
  • Nutritional guidance and lifestyle management

🏥 Medical Assistance Programs

Our medical assistance initiatives ensure that healthcare remains a basic right rather than a privilege.

  • Co-payment support for essential treatments
  • Subsidized medicines for chronic illnesses
  • Low-cost diagnostic tests and screenings
  • Health insurance awareness for low-income families

📚 Awareness & Education

Knowledge empowers prevention. Our programs focus on spreading awareness about health risks and preventive care.

  • Cancer awareness & prevention strategies
  • Healthy lifestyle education
  • Early symptom identification
  • Vaccination and routine checkup awareness

🧠 Patient Counseling & Rehabilitation

Emotional healing is as important as physical recovery. We support patients through counseling and rehabilitation programs.

  • Individual and group counseling
  • Post-surgical and post-cancer rehabilitation
  • Nutritional and lifestyle coaching
  • Support groups for families

🚫 Deaddiction Programs

Substance abuse affects families and communities. Our deaddiction programs help individuals reclaim healthy and meaningful lives.

  • Medical detoxification under expert care
  • Psychological counseling and therapy
  • Family support programs
  • Rehabilitation and reintegration support

💻 Virtual Training Programs

Our online health awareness and skill development courses are accessible anytime and anywhere, empowering individuals with knowledge for better health decisions.

  • Online health awareness courses
  • Skill development workshops
  • Live interactive sessions with healthcare experts
  • Self-paced learning modules for flexibility

🏕️ Wellness Camps

Comprehensive health and wellness camps designed to promote physical fitness, mental well-being, and healthy lifestyle practices across all age groups.

  • Physical fitness and exercise programs
  • Mental health and stress management workshops
  • Nutrition and dietary counseling
  • Community engagement and awareness activities
